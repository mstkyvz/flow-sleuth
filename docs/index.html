flow-sleuth

What moves between countries: military, state and civil aircraft, ships, trains and trade. Pick one or more countries and a day to see who flew in, out or over, from where to where, how much the countries trade, and whether their own figures agree. Open data only, no API keys.

Open-source intelligence from public broadcasts and official statistics. It shows aircraft, never passengers. Aircraft whose owners asked not to be tracked are never shown.

12 October 2026, military and state aircraft over Israel: US Air Force KC-135 tankers flying circuits out of Ben Gurion, C-17 transports from US bases in Germany, RAF aircraft, Israeli Air Force Gulfstream and C-130J.

Ask it

As a Claude Code skill, put the question in your own words. It runs the right commands and answers with a table, a map and the limits that apply.

  • Which military aircraft landed in Israel yesterday, and where did they come from?
  • Did any aircraft fly from Israel to Azerbaijan last week, and over which countries?
  • Compare trade between Azerbaijan and Israel as each side reports it.
  • Is anything flying over Syria right now?

What you get

One country, one day

Every military, state or civil flight in or over the country. Each one comes with its registration, aircraft type and full name, callsign, operator and state of registry, from → to airports, and the countries crossed in order. Click a flight to highlight its route. Open the example.

Routes between countries

From A to C, via B, with stops. Legs are joined into trips where the aircraft took off again from where it landed. Here: every direct flight between Israel and Azerbaijan on one day.

Relations report

Trade as each side reports it, with the gaps and mismatches between the two (embargoes, re-routing, confidential trade). Also the top products, ports and ship traffic, and flights between the countries. Israel · Azerbaijan · Syria.

Live map

Military aircraft worldwide, updated every 15 minutes on this site and live when run locally. Click an aircraft for where it took off and where it is going. Ships and trains appear where open live feeds exist. Open it.

What it takes into account

  • AircraftEvery aircraft the volunteer receivers of adsb.lol heard that day, with type, registration, operator and a military flag from the readsb database.
  • Who owns itThe state of registry, taken from the aircraft's 24-bit ICAO address block.
  • AirportsTake-off and landing when heard. When not heard: the airport where the track was lost low (probable), or the callsign's timetable route.
  • BordersThe countries each track crossed, in order, with entry and exit times.
  • TradeUN Comtrade figures as both partners report them: yearly, monthly and by product chapter.
  • ShipsIMF PortWatch port calls and seaborne tonnage from AIS, plus IMF's modelled trade between each port and a partner country.
  • Liveadsb.lol / adsb.fi aircraft, and Fintraffic Digitraffic ships and trains.
  • CoverageFor each country, how much traffic the receivers actually see. Before reading an absence as "nothing flew", check this.

What it cannot tell you

  • Who was on boardAircraft, never passengers. A flight from A to B says nothing about who flew.
  • Dark flightsMany military aircraft switch ADS-B off. Not seeing a flight does not mean it did not happen.
  • Thinly covered placesNear the ground, Syria, Iraq, Azerbaijan and much of China are poorly covered. The tool measures and shows this.
  • VerdictsThe relations report lists measurable signals. It does not say whether relations are good or bad.

Try it

git clone https://github.com/mstkyvz/flow-sleuth && cd flow-sleuth
uv run skills/flow-sleuth/scripts/day.py 2026-10-12
uv run skills/flow-sleuth/scripts/query.py country IL --date 2026-10-12 --class mil,state --map out/il.html
uv run skills/flow-sleuth/scripts/relations.py IL AZ SY --flights 2026-10-12:2026-10-12 --out out/il-az-sy
uv run skills/flow-sleuth/scripts/live.py --at 32.5,34.9
